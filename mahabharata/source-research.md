## Translation Challenge: Dharma

**The central untranslatable concept**: Dharma cannot simply be translated as "duty" or "law." It means:
- Cosmic order that sustains existence
- Individual's role in maintaining that order
- A binding, often unbreakable law that supersedes personal ethics
- Can command actions that seem immoral by Western standards
- Violation causes cosmic imbalance, not just social disorder

**Solution**: 
- Translate dharma literally when it appears
- Annotate it extensively with examples from the text
- Show how it operates in each context (cosmic, social, individual)
- Demonstrate why characters cannot act against it even when it causes suffering

**Extended cosmology section is essential** because without understanding that:
1. Time is cyclical (so this has happened before and will again)
2. Reincarnation is real (so Draupadi's suffering has cosmic cause in past karma)
3. Dharma is cosmic law (so violating it would destroy more than just honor)

...the Draupadi scene will read as mere brutality rather than cosmic tragedy.
