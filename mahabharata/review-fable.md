## 1. Sanskrit correctness of the commentary

1. L504/CLAUDE.md — *anṛtau* (1.58.6c `ṛtāv ṛtau … na kāmān nānṛtau tathā`). **Correct.** Locative of *anṛtu* "non-season", parallel to the preceding *ṛtāv ṛtau*; *anṛta* "untruth" would give *anṛte*. No pun.
2. L1018 — *prakṛti* pl. as the constituents of the realm; seven-element list (king, ministers, territory with people, fort, treasury, army, ally). **Correct** (Arthaśāstra *saptāṅga*: svāmin, amātya, janapada, durga, kośa, daṇḍa, mitra). The admission that the syntax of 2.45.13ab is loose is right: *abhibhūya* (gerund) with *pare sthitāḥ* (nom. pl.) as its agent is the only way to keep *svāḥ prakṛtīḥ* as object; the page's reading is the natural one.
3. L2029 — *Dharmapāśasitaḥ* = dharma + pāśa + sita. **Correct** (*sita*, p.p.p. of √si/sā "bind"). The cross-reference is **imprecise**: 2.61.67 has *pāśān* but not *sita*; only 2.52.18 (`pāśair iva naraḥ sitaḥ`) shares both. Replace: "It shares *pāśa* with Varuṇa's nooses (2.61.67) and both *pāśa* and *sita* with 2.52.18; cf. also *dharmapāśānubaddhaḥ* of Bhīma at 2.63.6."
4. L466, L563 — *dharā* / *dhārayitum* / *dharma* all from √dhṛ. **Correct.** *dhārayitum* is the infinitive of the causative *dhārayati*; citation form without sandhi is proper in commentary (the Haiku objection is rejected, see §6.10).
5. L1723 — *Rajasvalā* = menstruating woman; "covered with dust" also attested (*rajasvala*). **Correct.** The seclusion/impurity note is accurate.
6. L1857 — *bandhakī*: attested sense "unchaste woman, harlot"; "tied to many men" marked as a guess from the root; Bhīma's plural *bandhakyaḥ* at 2.61.1a. **Correct**, including the hedge. Note only: the same word is rendered "prostitutes" in the Bhīma paraphrase (L1802) but "whore" in Karṇa's verse; make one choice (see §2.31).
7. L466 — *aṃśāvataraṇa* "descent by portions". **Correct** (1.61.99c, 101c, 102a `aṃśāvataraṇam`).
8. L950 — *pauruṣa* built on *puruṣa*. **Correct** (taddhita with vṛddhi). The arc 2.43.29 → 2.45.13 is a fair reading.
9. L710 — *strīpuṃsa* "woman-and-man" (1.61.87c `strīpuṃsaṃ`). **Correct** as a dvandva; the word is also the ordinary term for a hermaphrodite, which is what is meant of Śikhaṇḍin. Optional: "woman-and-man, i.e. of both sexes".
10. L1233 — *draupadīm ādikṛtvā* "putting Draupadī at their head". **Correct** gloss (gerund of *ādi-kṛ* "make first"), but the verse is 2.52.17c, not within the block 2.52.14–16 it sits under. Add "(2.52.17)".
11. L884 — *apsv iva nīrajāḥ* "like lotuses in water". Translation **correct**; placement **error**. The line is 1.115.28c (`te ca pañca śataṃ caiva … sarve vavṛdhur alpena kālenāpsv iva nīrajāḥ`), the Pāṇḍavas and Kauravas growing up on the Himālaya — it does not "follow in the source" after 2.43.6. Move the note to the end of the 1.115 section (after L845) and cite 1.115.27–28; delete from L884.
12. L1534 — *śriyas tu bhāgaḥ* "a portion of Śrī". **Correct** (gen. *śriyaḥ* + *bhāgaḥ*, 1.61.95a).
13. L434 — *dharmo hi hato hanti* "dharma, struck down, strikes down". **Correct.**
14. L435 — *tasya pāpam* ambiguous (objective vs subjective genitive). **Correct**; both readings are real.
15. L627 — "went … to Nārāyaṇa at Vaikuṇṭha". **Imprecise.** 1.58.49c `nārāyaṇam amitraghnaṃ vaikuṇṭham upacakramuḥ`: *vaikuṇṭham* is accusative in apposition, an epithet of Nārāyaṇa, not a place. Replace: "to Nārāyaṇa, the slayer of foes, Vaikuṇṭha himself".
16. L564 — 1.58.34c `abrahmaṇyā vīryamadā mattā madabalena ca` rendered "drunk on strength and drunk on the drunkenness of strength". **Imprecise.** *vīryamadāḥ* (bahuvrīhi) "whose intoxication was their valour"; *madabalena* "by the force of their intoxication/arrogance". Replace: "irreverent, intoxicated with their valour, mad with the force of that intoxication".
17. L799 — 1.114.8c `prāhuḥ kṣatraṃ balajyeṣṭhaṃ balajyeṣṭhaṃ sutaṃ vṛṇu` rendered "has strength for its eldest … whose eldest quality is strength". **Imprecise.** *jyeṣṭha* here is "foremost, pre-eminent"; the "eldest" pun is forced and "eldest quality" is not English. Replace: "They say the warrior class is foremost in strength; choose a son foremost in strength."
18. L1215 — 2.52.13a `rājātidevī kṛtahasto matākṣaḥ` rendered "a king who plays excessively, with a made hand, an expert with dice". **Imprecise.** *atidevin* = a consummate/over-the-top gambler; *kṛtahasta* = dexterous, practised of hand (not "made hand"); *matākṣa* = knowing the dice. Replace: "a king who is a consummate gambler, practised of hand, who knows the dice".
19. L1349 — 2.54.2c `hiraṇyam akṣayyaṃ jātarūpam anekaśaḥ` rendered "an inexhaustible treasury of wrought gold". **Imprecise.** *jātarūpa* is gold in its native state (unwrought); *hiraṇya* is the bullion. Replace: "an inexhaustible treasury of gold, native gold in abundance".
20. L1247 — *prajñācakṣus* "whose eye is his intelligence". **Correct.**
21. L493 — *triḥsaptakṛtvaḥ* = twenty-one times. **Correct.**
22. L796 — "Indra's asterism" (*aindra* = Jyeṣṭhā), "eighth muhūrta, Abhijit", midday. **Correct.**
23. L677 — Dvāpara third yuga; dice throws kṛta/tretā/dvāpara/kali with *kali* the losing throw. **Correct** as lexical fact (Vedic dicing terms); it is extra-textual and the Haiku "not located" is expected. Optional: add "(the names of the four throws in the old dice game)".
24. L648 — "Kālakeya". **Error** (name form). The text has *kāleyānām* (1.61.46c, 53a); the mother is *kālakā* (45a). Print "Kāleya".

## 2. Fidelity of verse-trans to the Sanskrit

Read all 113 blocks. Only departures are listed.

1. L554 (1.58.25–28) — **imprecision**. The Sanskrit block marks the omitted verse 27 with "…", but the English runs 26cd straight into 28 ("they came to be here on the earth — among cattle and horses…"). Insert "…" after "here on the earth" or translate 27 (`iha devatvam icchanto mānuṣeṣu manasvinaḥ / jajñire bhuvi bhūteṣu teṣu teṣv asurā vibho`: "Wanting godhead here among men, the proud asuras were born on earth, lord, in this and that kind of being —").
2. L744 (1.113.28, 30) — **imprecision** (omitted vocatives). *anavadyāṅgi* (28c), *sukeśānte* (30a), *pṛthuśroṇi* (30e) are all dropped; *guṇasamāyuktān* is "endowed with qualities", not "every quality". Replace: "…as I am, woman of flawless limbs, longing to look at a son. … At my appointment, then, fair-tressed one, by a twice-born man superior in austerity, you should bring forth sons endowed with good qualities. Through you, broad-hipped one, I would go to the destination of those who have sons."
3. L778 (1.113.39–40) — **note**. *varārohe* (39a, 40c) dropped twice. Optional: "fair-hipped one".
4. L825 (1.114.16c) — **imprecision**/consistency. *vidhinā* rendered "rite"; the project renders *vidhi* "ordinance" (2.53.13 "Ordinance is strong"). Either is possible here (ritual prescription), but say so or harmonise: "Of these, daiva is obtained by ordinance joined with the right time."
5. L880 (2.43.3–5) — fine. But gloss L883 (2.43.10) — **error**. `dvāraṃ ca vivṛtākāraṃ lalāṭena samāhanat / saṃvṛtaṃ ceti manvāno dvāradeśād upāramat`: he struck his forehead on a door that *looked* open (and was shut), and drew back from another doorway thinking it shut. Page: "walked into a doorway that was open, thinking it shut, and struck it with his forehead" is self-contradictory. Replace: "then he struck his forehead on a door that looked open, and shied back from a doorway that was open, thinking it shut."
6. L916 (2.43.23) — **note**. `kṣāntavanto 'parādhaṃ tam` = "forgave that offence [Śiśupāla's killing]"; "enduring it" loses the object. Optional: "kings themselves burnt by the fire that rises off the Pāṇḍavas, yet forgiving the offence".
7. L934 (2.43.29c) — **note**. "having come to another": *āgatām* is just "arrived"; "to another" is supplied. Acceptable; or "a fortune of that sort that has come about".
8. L1014 (2.45.13) — reading defended in §1.2; **no change**.
9. L1017 (2.45.17–27 inventory) — **error** in the last clause. 2.45.24–25 `brāhmaṇā vāṭadhānāś ca gomantaḥ śatasaṃghaśaḥ / traikharvaṃ balim ādāya dvāri tiṣṭhanti vāritāḥ / kamaṇḍalūn upādāya jātarūpamayāñ śubhān / evaṃ baliṃ samādāya praveśaṃ lebhire tataḥ`: brahmins *and* Vāṭadhānas (two groups), cattle-owners, in hundreds of bands, bringing a tribute of three kharvas, stood at the gate kept out; only when they came carrying fine golden water-pots as tribute did they get in. Replace: "brahmins and cattle-rich Vāṭadhānas, in bands of hundreds, kept waiting at the gate with a tribute of three kharvas, and admitted only once they had brought golden water-pots as their offering." ("three billion" is also doubtful: *kharva* is 10^10 in the standard series; print "three kharvas".) Minor in the same gloss: 2.45.19 lists three colours (`kṛṣṇaśyāmāruṇāni`, "black, dark and red"), not "dark-red"; 2.45.20 *yoṣit* (women) is dropped and *vāmī* is "mare/she-camel", not "she-ass".
10. L1096 (2.45.54c) — **imprecision**/consistency. *diṣṭam* rendered "apportioned"; the project's decision is *diṣṭa* → "appointed" (so at 2.52.14, 2.53.13). Replace: "This is what has been appointed; no doubt of it."
11. L1111 (2.51.13a) — **note**. *naḥ* ("for us who play") dropped: "The door of heaven is a distinguished one for us who play".
12. L1184 (2.52.7–9) — **imprecision** (quotation boundary). The *iti* at 9c closes the king's message, so 9ab–c ("the gamblers appointed there … you will see them, seated") is still the king's speech, not Vidura's own remark. Move the closing quotation mark after "seated."
13. L1230 (2.52.14–16) — fine. (*māyopadhāḥ* "whose foundation is trickery" acceptable; "fraud and trickery their means" is closer.)
14. L1260 (2.52.31c) — **note**. `dadṛśuś copayātās tān draupadīpramukhāḥ striyaḥ`: *upayātāḥ* = "who had come [along]", not "came out". Alternative: "and the women, Draupadī at their head, who had come with them, looked on."
15. L1331 (2.53.16a) — **imprecision**. `anyenānyasya … devanam` is play *by* one man *on behalf of* another (Śakuni for Duryodhana, 2.53.15), not "with another man's stake". Replace: "For one man to play on another's behalf seems to me irregular."
16. L1334 (gloss, 2.53.18c) — **imprecision**. *nātīvaprītamanasaḥ* = "not very glad", exactly as the page renders *nātipramanasaḥ* at L1260. Replace "not at all glad" with "not very glad".
17. L1372 (gloss, 2.58.5c) — **imprecision**. `prāk sindhor` = "east of the Sindhu", not "as far as the Sindhu"; *anuvarṇānām* = "of the lower classes" rather than "every class". Replace: "belonging to the lesser classes east of the Sindhu".
18. L1414 (gloss, 2.58.12a) — **note**. *rājaputraḥ* goes more naturally with Nakula: "Prince Nakula is dear to you, king, Yudhiṣṭhira."
19. L1482 (2.58.27c) — **imprecision**. `kuryāmas te jitāḥ karma svayam ātmany upaplave`: *svayam* ("I myself [am the stake]") is lost. Replace: "I stake myself: won, we would do your work ourselves, in our own ruin."

Replace: "With Yudhiṣṭhira drunk on the madness of dice, Duryodhana has won you, Draupadī. Come to Dhṛtarāṣṭra's house; I am taking you to your work, Yājñasenī."

## 5. Memorization check

No sentence-level match to any published translation was found; renderings are independent in syntax and word choice throughout. Points of lexical overlap with van Buitenen's fixed vocabulary, for the record (from memory, not verified against the printed volumes):

1. "Steward" for *kṣattṛ* (L1047, 1096, 1166, 1198, 1590, 1607) — van Buitenen's standard rendering; the most recognisable tic on the page. **Note**: consider "Kṣattṛ" untranslated, with a one-line gloss, or "Chamberlain".
2. "Wolf-belly" for Vṛkodara (L825, 2045) — also van Buitenen's ("Wolf-Belly"); but it is the literal gloss and Ganguli leaves it untranslated. **Note** only.
