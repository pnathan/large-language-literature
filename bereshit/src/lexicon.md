# Bereshit 1:1–4:18 — lexicon of head terms

One English head term per Hebrew root. Where the English departs from the head
term, the alignment names the reason. The LXX column is the ancient Greek
rendering as a witness to the sense the translators took.

| Hebrew | Root | Head term (English) | LXX | Attested range (this span) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| bara' | ב-ר-א | cut | epoiēsen (made) | create / cut / shape | Root disputed; "cut" is the physical sense kept throughout. "Created" is the settled English and is rejected for register. |
| 'asah | ע-ש-ה | make | epoiēsen | make / do | Plain verb for ordinary making, including the firmament (1:7). |
| 'amar / vayomer | א-מ-ר | said | eipen | say | Speech formula, all speakers. No "howled" or "hissed" in the formula. |
| ra'ah | ר-א-ה | saw | eiden | see | Formula "saw: good". |
| tov | ט-ו-ב | good | kalon | good / pleasing | Formula; "very good" for me'od. |
| yada' | י-ד-ע | knew | egnō | know / intimately know | Rule: "knew" for the narrative past; "do not know" for the negated 4:9. Intimate sense (4:1, 4:17) is marked in the notes, not in the English word. |
| sha'a | ש-ע-ה | looked | epeiden | look upon / gaze | Used of God's regard for Hevel and Qayin (4:4–5). |
| 'erets | א-ר-ץ | ground | gē | earth, ground, land | Rule: "ground" for every 'erets in the text; "land" only for the place names Nod, Havilah, Kush. Applied at 1:22, 1:28, 2:6, 4:12, 4:14. |
| adamah | א-ד-מ-ה | the red | gē | ground, soil | "The red" is a stretch kept for the pun with adam; the commentary says so. |
| adam | א-ד-מ | the human | anthrōpos | human, man | "The human" is used for adam; 'ish is "man"; 'ishah is "woman". |
| qanah | ק-נ-ה | got | ektēsamēn | acquire, create | Only use in this span: 4:1 "I have gotten a man." |
| nefesh | נ-פ-ש | living breath / living throat | psychē | breath, throat, self | Stretched to the bodily throat at 2:7 only. |
| ruach | ר-ו-ח | wind | pneuma | wind, breath, spirit | "Wind" at 1:2 and 3:8. |
| qavah | ק-ו-ה | pool / hope | — | gather, wait, hope | Two English readings kept at 1:9–1:10. |
| badal | ב-ד-ל | cut the seam / split | diachōrizō | separate | "Cut a seam" at 1:4, 1:18; "split" for the waters at 1:7. |
| nathan | נ-ת-נ | give | didōmi | give, set, put | Rule: "give" for every nathan. Applied at 1:17 ("gave them a place"), 1:29, 3:12. |

Rule for contested terms: the English carries an English gloss, never raw Hebrew
or transliteration. The Hebrew token itself goes in the apparatus, linked to the
English unit it renders, so each gloss can be checked against the Hebrew.

Decided: ruach is 'wind' at 1:2 and 3:8 (the sense 'spirit' is noted only).
bara' is 'cut' throughout (create, carve, hack and hew are noted as alternatives).
nefesh is 'living breath' at 2:7 (the sense 'living throat' is noted).
moadim at 1:14 is 'seasons', with the ritual-time sense in the note.

Still open: the commentary and notes still describe some of these terms by their
English gloss and have not been re-audited against this rule. Also open: the
per-verse check that each head term is used where it appears.
