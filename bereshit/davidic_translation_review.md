# Davidic-Era Oral Translation & Philological Review
## Genesis 1:1–4:18 (Bereshit)

### Introduction: The Oral Horizon of Early Monarchic Judah (c. 10th Century BCE)

To translate Genesis 1:1–4:18 as it might have sounded when recited in the court of David haMelech in Hebron or Jerusalem is to strip away millennia of post-exilic dogmatics, Greek philosophical abstractions, and later Rabbinic commentary. In the 10th century BCE, Hebrew was a vivid, physical, concrete, Northwest Semitic dialect—closely allied with Moabite, Phoenician, and Ugaritic—spoken in a society structured by clan feuds (*go'el ha-dam*), sanctuary boundary rites, royal-sacred gardens, and animal offerings (*minchah*).

#### Key Features of the Davidic Oral Style:

1. **Paratactic Cadence (*Vav*-Consecutive):**
   The story moves in a continuous, driving rhythmic sequence (*"And... and... and..."*). This is the voice of the *moshid* (oral singer of tales) performing before an audience, using repetitive formulas and oral cues to anchor the narrative in memory.

2. **Concrete Physicality over Philosophical Abstraction:**
   - *Bara*: Not *creatio ex nihilo*, but cutting, carving, clearing space (cognate to *beri'ah* in Josh 17:15).
   - *Tohu va-vohu*: Wilder-waste and empty void—a desolate, wind-swept chaos.
   - *Tehom*: The roaring watery abyss (cognate to Ugaritic *thm* and Akkadian *Tiamat*).
   - *Ruach Elohim*: A fierce, vibrating divine wind/blast sweeping across the dark waters.
   - *Raqi'a*: A physical, beaten metal vault or hammered plate (*raqa* = to hammer out metal) separating cosmic waters above from below.
   - *Adam / Adamah*: The "earthling" molded from "red soil"—a direct wordplay on physical origins.
   - *Neshamah*: A direct blast of living breath into the nostrils (*'appayim*).
   - *'Ezer keneghdo*: A "matching ally" or "counter-strength", an equal partner standing face-to-face.
   - *'Atzem me'atzamay*: "Bone of my bones, flesh of my flesh"—a sacred tribal covenant oath (cf. 2 Sam 5:1).

3. **Ancient Near Eastern Ritual & Cosmic Polemics:**
   - **Aniconic Sun & Moon:** The sun and moon are intentionally not named by their pagan deity names (*shemesh*, *yareach*), but called "the big light" and "the small light"—relegating them to ritual timekeepers (*mo'adim*) for the cultic calendar.
   - **The Royal Sanctuary (Eden):** Eden is portrayed as the King's sacred walled garden at the headwaters of the subterranean abyss (*ed*), guarded by *Keruvim*—winged sphinx-guardians—and a spinning blade.
   - **Sacrificial Demands & Lurking Demons:** Cain's grain gift versus Abel's fat animal offering. Sin (*chatat*) is personified as a *rovetz*—a crouching demon/beast at the door (Akkadian *rabisu*). Spilled blood (*damim*, plural) pollutes the soil and screams for blood-vengeance.

### Genesis Chapter 1

**1:1** At the primary cutting when God carved out the heavens and the earth—
**1:2** the earth was wilder-waste and empty void, with darkness over the face of the roaring deep, and the blast of God vibrating over the face of the waters.
**1:3** And God said, "Let light be!" and light was.
**1:4** And God saw the light, that it was good; and God severed between the light and the darkness.
**1:5** And God called out to the light, "Day!" and to the darkness he called out, "Night!" And evening came, and morning came: day one.

**1:6** And God said, "Let there be a beaten vault in the midst of the waters, and let it sever between waters and waters!"
**1:7** And God fashioned the beaten vault, and severed between the waters under the vault and the waters above the vault; and it was so.
**1:8** And God called out to the vault, "Heavens!" And evening came, and morning came: a second day.

**1:9** And God said, "Let the waters under the heavens be gathered to one place, and let the dry ground be seen!" and it was so.
**1:10** And God called out to the dry ground, "Earth!" and to the gathering of waters he called out, "Seas!" And God saw that it was good.
**1:11** And God said, "Let the earth sprout green shoots: seed-yielding herbs, fruit trees yielding fruit after their kind with their seed inside them upon the earth!" and it was so.
**1:12** And the earth brought forth green shoots, herbs yielding seed after their kind, and trees yielding fruit with their seed inside them after their kind; and God saw that it was good.
**1:13** And evening came, and morning came: a third day.

**1:14** And God said, "Let there be light-bearers in the beaten vault of the heavens to sever between the day and the night, and let them serve as markers for appointed seasons, for days and for years!
**1:15** And let them be light-bearers in the beaten vault of the heavens to give light upon the earth!" and it was so.
**1:16** And God fashioned the two great light-bearers: the larger light-bearer to hold sway over the day, and the smaller light-bearer to hold sway over the night, and the stars.
**1:17** And God set them in the beaten vault of the heavens to give light upon the earth,
**1:18** and to hold sway over the day and over the night, and to sever between the light and the darkness; and God saw that it was good.
**1:19** And evening came, and morning came: a fourth day.

**1:20** And God said, "Let the waters swarm with swarms of living breath, and let flying creatures fly above the earth across the face of the vault of the heavens!"
**1:21** And God carved out the great sea-monsters and every living breathing crawler with which the waters swarmed after their kinds, and every winged flyer after its kind; and God saw that it was good.
**1:22** And God blessed them, saying, "Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the waters in the seas, and let the flyers multiply on the earth!"
**1:23** And evening came, and morning came: a fifth day.

**1:24** And God said, "Let the earth bring forth living breath after its kind: cattle, crawling things, and wild beasts of the earth after their kind!" and it was so.
**1:25** And God fashioned the wild beasts of the earth after their kind, and the cattle after their kind, and every crawler of the soil after its kind; and God saw that it was good.

**1:26** And God said, "Let us make an earthling in our image, according to our likeness, and let them hold sway over the fish of the sea, over the flyers of the heavens, over the cattle, over all the earth, and over every crawler that crawls upon the earth!"
**1:27** And God carved out the earthling in his image: in the image of God he carved him out; male and female he carved them out.
**1:28** And God blessed them, and God said to them, "Be fruitful and multiply, fill the earth and conquer it, and hold sway over the fish of the sea, the flyers of the heavens, and every living creature that crawls upon the earth!"
**1:29** And God said, "Look! I have given you every seed-bearing herb upon the face of all the earth, and every tree that has seed-bearing fruit; for you it shall be for food.
**1:30** And to every wild beast of the earth, to every flyer of the heavens, and to everything crawling upon the earth that has living breath in it, I give every green herb for food!" and it was so.
**1:31** And God saw all that he had fashioned, and look—it was exceedingly good! And evening came, and morning came: the sixth day.

### Genesis Chapter 2

**2:1** Thus were finished the heavens and the earth, and all their host.
**2:2** And on the seventh day God completed his work which he had done, and he ceased on the seventh day from all his work which he had done.
**2:3** And God blessed the seventh day and set it apart as holy, because on it he ceased from all his work which God had carved out in the making.

**2:4** This is the clan-table of the heavens and the earth when they were carved out, on the day the LORD God made earth and heavens—
**2:5** before any wild shrub was yet on the earth, and before any herb of the field had sprouted, for the LORD God had not rained upon the earth, and there was no earthling to work the soil.
**2:6** But a subterranean surge would well up from the earth and drench the whole face of the soil.
**2:7** Then the LORD God molded the earthling out of dust from the red soil, and blew into his nostrils the blast of living breath, and the earthling became a living breathing throat.

**2:8** And the LORD God planted a garden in Eden, away in the east, and there he put the earthling whom he had molded.
**2:9** And the LORD God caused to sprout from the soil every tree desirable to the sight and good for food, with the tree of life in the midst of the garden, and the tree of knowing good and evil.
**2:10** Now a river flows out from Eden to drench the garden, and from there it splits and becomes four headwaters:
**2:11** The name of the first is Pishon; it winds through the whole land of Havilah, where there is gold—
**2:12** and the gold of that land is fine; aromatic gum and lapis stone are there.
**2:13** The name of the second river is Gihon; it winds through the whole land of Cush.
**2:14** The name of the third river is Tigris; it flows east of Asshur. And the fourth river is the Euphrates.

**2:15** And the LORD God took the earthling and placed him in the garden of Eden to work it and to guard it.
**2:16** And the LORD God commanded the earthling, saying, "From every tree of the garden you may surely eat,
**2:17** but from the tree of knowing good and evil you shall not eat from it, for on the day you eat from it, dying you will die!"

**2:18** And the LORD God said, "It is not good for the earthling to be alone. I will make for him a matching ally."
**2:19** Out of the soil the LORD God had molded every beast of the field and every flyer of the heavens, and brought them to the earthling to see what he would call out to them; and whatever the earthling called out to each living creature, that became its name.
**2:20** So the earthling called out names for all the cattle, for the flyers of the heavens, and for every beast of the field; but for the earthling there was found no matching ally.

**2:21** So the LORD God caused a heavy sleep to fall upon the earthling, and he slept; and he took one of his ribs and closed up the flesh beneath it.
**2:22** And the LORD God built the rib which he had taken from the earthling into a woman, and brought her to the earthling.
**2:23** And the earthling said:
"This one, this time!
Bone of my bones,
and flesh of my flesh!
She shall be called Woman (*Ishah*),
for from Man (*Ish*) she was taken!"
**2:24** Therefore a man leaves his father and his mother and clings to his woman, and they become one flesh.
**2:25** And they were both naked-skinned, the earthling and his woman, and felt no shame.

### Genesis Chapter 3

**3:1** Now the serpent was craftier than any wild beast of the field that the LORD God had made. And it said to the woman, "Did God really say, 'You shall not eat from any tree of the garden'?"
**3:2** And the woman said to the serpent, "From the fruit of the garden trees we may eat;
**3:3** but from the fruit of the tree in the midst of the garden, God said: 'You shall not eat from it, nor shall you touch it, lest you die!'"
**3:4** And the serpent said to the woman, "Dying, you will not die!
**3:5** For God knows that on the day you eat from it, your eyes will be opened, and you will be like gods, knowing good and evil!"

**3:6** When the woman saw that the tree was good for food, that it was a delight to the eyes, and desirable for making one wise, she took of its fruit and ate; and she also gave to her man beside her, and he ate.
**3:7** Then the eyes of both were opened, and they knew that they were naked; so they sewed fig leaves together and made themselves waist-girdles.

**3:8** And they heard the sound of the LORD God pacing about in the garden in the breeze of the day, and the earthling and his woman hid themselves from the face of the LORD God among the trees of the garden.
**3:9** And the LORD God called out to the earthling and said to him, "Where are you?"
**3:10** And he said, "I heard your sound in the garden, and I was afraid because I am naked, so I hid."
**3:11** And he said, "Who told you that you were naked? Have you eaten from the tree from which I commanded you not to eat?"
**3:12** And the earthling said, "The woman whom you gave to be with me—she gave me from the tree, and I ate."
**3:13** And the LORD God said to the woman, "What is this you have done?" And the woman said, "The serpent beguiled me, and I ate."

**3:14** And the LORD God said to the serpent:
"Because you have done this,
accursed are you above all cattle
and above every beast of the field!
Upon your belly you shall crawl,
and dust you shall eat all the days of your life!
**3:15** I will set enmity between you and the woman,
and between your offspring and her offspring;
he will strike at your head,
and you will strike at his heel!"

**3:16** To the woman he said:
"I will greatly multiply your agony and your pregnancies;
in pain you shall bear children.
Your thirst shall be for your man,
and he shall rule over you."

**3:17** And to the earthling he said:
"Because you listened to the voice of your woman and ate from the tree about which I commanded you, saying, 'You shall not eat from it'—
accursed is the soil on your account!
In agony you shall eat from it all the days of your life.
**3:18** Thorns and thistles it shall sprout for you,
and you shall eat the herbs of the field.
**3:19** By the sweat of your brow you shall eat bread
until you return to the red soil,
for from it you were taken;
for dust you are,
and to dust you shall return!"

**3:20** And the earthling called his woman's name Eve (*Chavah*), because she was the mother of all living (*chai*).
**3:21** And the LORD God made for the earthling and his woman tunic-garments of skin, and clothed them.

**3:22** And the LORD God said, "Look—the earthling has become like one of us, knowing good and evil! Now, lest he stretch out his hand and take also from the tree of life, and eat, and live forever—"
**3:23** So the LORD God sent him away from the garden of Eden to work the red soil from which he was taken.
**3:24** He drove out the earthling, and stationed east of the garden of Eden the *Keruvim* and the flashing, spinning blade of the sword to guard the way to the tree of life.

### Genesis Chapter 4 (4:1–4:18)

**4:1** Now the earthling knew Eve his woman, and she conceived and bore Cain (*Qayin*), and said, "I have acquired (*qaniti*) a man-child with the LORD!"
**4:2** And she bore again, his brother Abel (*Hevel*). Now Abel became a herder of sheep, and Cain became a worker of the soil.

**4:3** And in the course of time, Cain brought from the fruit of the soil a tribute-offering (*minchah*) to the LORD.
**4:4** And Abel, he also brought from the firstborn of his flock and from their fat portions. And the LORD looked favorably upon Abel and upon his tribute-offering,
**4:5** but upon Cain and his tribute-offering he did not look favorably. So Cain burned with fury, and his face fell.

**4:6** And the LORD said to Cain, "Why are you furious, and why has your face fallen?
**4:7** If you do well, is there not uplifting? But if you do not do well, sin is a crouching demon (*rovetz*) at the door; its thirst is for you, but you must master it!"

**4:8** And Cain spoke to Abel his brother... And when they were in the field, Cain rose up against Abel his brother and slaughtered him.
**4:9** And the LORD said to Cain, "Where is Abel your brother?" And he said, "I do not know. Am I my brother's keeper?"
**4:10** And he said, "What have you done? The voice of your brother's bloods is screaming to me from the red soil!
**4:11** So now, accursed are you from the soil, which opened its mouth to receive your brother's bloods from your hand!
**4:12** When you work the soil, it will no longer yield its strength to you; a homeless wanderer you shall be on the earth!"

**4:13** And Cain said to the LORD, "My punishment is too great to bear!
**4:14** Look, you have driven me today from the face of the soil, and from your face I shall be hidden; I shall be a homeless wanderer on the earth, and whoever finds me will kill me!"
**4:15** And the LORD said to him, "Not so! Whoever kills Cain, sevenfold vengeance shall be taken on him!" And the LORD set a mark upon Cain, so that no one finding him would strike him down.

**4:16** Then Cain went out from the presence of the LORD and dwelt in the land of Nod (Wandering), east of Eden.
**4:17** And Cain knew his woman, and she conceived and bore Enoch; and he built a fortified settlement, and named the settlement after his son Enoch.
**4:18** And to Enoch was born Irad; and Irad fathered Mehujael, and Mehujael fathered Methushael, and Methushael fathered Lamech.

### Historical & Anthropological Reflection

1. **Courtly vs. Folk Registers:**
   In Davidic Jerusalem, this story sat at the intersection of tribal campfire oral tradition and early monarchic court scribal culture. The formal royal decrees in Chapter 1 reflect royal-cultic proclamations, while Chapter 2–4 represent ancient folk etiology, clan blood-feud law, and sanctuary boundary lore.

2. **The Metaphor of Blood and Soil:**
   In 10th-century Judah, murder (*damim*, plural "bloods") was not merely an infraction against moral law; it was a physical pollution of the land. The red soil (*adamah*) physically swallows the spilled blood, rendering the earth barren to the perpetrator and forcing him into outlawry (*Nod*). The mark (*'ot*) functions as an ancient clan sign of asylum, enforcing blood-vengeance restrictions.

3. **Demonic & Ritual Terminology:**
   The term *rovetz* in 4:7 directly preserves the Mesopotamian/West Semitic demonological concept of the *rabisu*—a predatory threshold demon waiting at the entry of the dwelling.
