# Names table, printed after chapter 4. One entry per line: Hebrew | English form | meaning.
# Meanings use Hebrew script for Hebrew words; no transliteration.

אָדָם | the human | from אֲדָמָה, 'ground'. The link is the text's own wordplay (2:7), not an established etymology.
חַוָּה | Chavah | from חַי, 'living'. The link is the text's own (3:20); the form is unusual.
קַיִן | Qayin | the text links it to קָנִיתִי, 'I have acquired' (4:1). A proposed sense 'smith, spear' is not settled.
הֶבֶל | Hevel | 'breath, vapour': the word of Ecclesiastes for what does not last.
עֵדֶן | Eden | heard as 'delight'; a loan from Sumerian 'steppe' is also proposed. Disputed.
נוֹד | Nod | 'wandering', from the root of נָד (4:12, 4:14). Qayin is sent there.
חֲנוֹךְ | Chanokh | 'dedicated, trained', from חָנַךְ. Uncertain.
עִירָד | Irad | unclear; 'wild ass' and a place name are proposed.
מְחוּיָאֵל | Mechuyael | written מְחִיָּיאֵל in the same verse. 'Smitten by אֵל' or 'given life by אֵל'; contested.
מְתוּשָׁאֵל | Methushael | 'man of אֵל' is proposed; a link with מוֹת, 'death', is also proposed. Disputed.
לָמֶךְ | Lamekh | unclear.
אִשָּׁה / אִישׁ | Ishah / Ish | 'woman / man'. The pun is the text's own (2:23); one root is not established. The English keeps the Hebrew words at 2:23.
חֲוִילָה | Havilah | unidentified land; a link with חוּל, 'to writhe', is proposed and not established.
פִּישׁוֹן / גִּיחוֹן | Pishon / Gihon | Pishon is unidentified (Rashi: the Nile). Gihon may be from a root 'to burst forth'; not settled.
חִדֶּקֶל / פְּרָת | Hiddekel / Perat | the Tigris and the Euphrates.
כּוּשׁ / אַשּׁוּר | Kush / Ashur | a southern land (Nubia or Ethiopia), and Assyria.
