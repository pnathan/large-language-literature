# Four Complete Hermeneutical Rewrites of Genesis 1–3

This document contains full, verse-by-verse translations of Genesis Chapters 1, 2, and 3 rendered according to four distinct, historical hermeneutical traditions.

# I. The Gnostic / Sethian Archontic Hermeneutic
*Reflecting 2nd–3rd Century CE Sethian and Valentinian Gnostic cosmic inverse texts (Apocryphon of John, Hypostasis of the Archons, On the Origin of the World).*

## Genesis Chapter 1
**1:1** In the archontic realm of beginning, the Blind Demiurge Yaldabaoth carved out the physical skies and the material prison of earth.
**1:2** And the earth was a dark, formless void of ignorance, with abyss-gloom covering the face of the matter-deep, while the sorrowful Divine Spirit (*Pneuma/Sophia*) hovered above the waters, grieving for her fallen spark.
**1:3** And the Demiurge proclaimed in his arrogance, "Let there be light!" and a stolen ray of divine light was trapped in matter.
**1:4** And the Demiurge saw the light, that it was powerful, and the Demiurge severed the light from the surrounding darkness.
**1:5** And the Demiurge called the light "Day," and the darkness he called "Night." And evening came, and morning came: day one of the cosmic entrapment.
**1:6** And the Demiurge said, "Let there be a firm vault in the midst of the material waters, to divide the lower waters from the upper realm!"
**1:7** And the Demiurge fashioned the physical vault, severing the abyssal waters below from the waters above the vault; and so it stood.
**1:8** And the Demiurge called the vault "Heavens." And evening came, and morning came: a second day.
**1:9** And the Demiurge said, "Let the waters under the heavens be gathered into one basin, and let the dry land appear!" and so it stood.
**1:10** And the Demiurge called the dry land "Earth," and the collection of waters he called "Seas"; and the Demiurge looked upon his material realm and pronounced it good.
**1:11** And the Demiurge said, "Let the earth sprout physical vegetation, seed-bearing herbs, and fruit trees yielding fruit according to their dense material kinds!" and so it stood.
**1:12** And the earth brought forth material vegetation and seed-yielding plants after their kind; and the Demiurge saw that his physical structure functioned.
**1:13** And evening came, and morning came: a third day.
**1:14** And the Demiurge said, "Let there be cosmic rulers (*Archons*) as light-givers in the vault of heaven to sever day from night, and let them serve as fatalistic markers for horoscopes, seasons, days, and years!
**1:15** Let them shine in the metal vault of heaven to govern the earth below!" and so it stood.
**1:16** And the Demiurge fashioned the two great astral Archons: the larger ruler (*Hebdomad/Sun*) to govern the day, and the smaller ruler (*Moon*) to govern the night, along with the star-rulers (*Fate/Heimarmene*).
**1:17** And the Demiurge set them in the vault of heaven to rule over the earth,
**1:18** to hold sway over day and night and enforce the cosmic enclosure; and the Demiurge approved his fatalistic machine.
**1:19** And evening came, and morning came: a fourth day.
**1:20** And the Demiurge said, "Let the waters swarm with unreasoning, mortal creatures, and let winged flyers flit across the face of the physical sky-vault!"
**1:21** And the Demiurge created the great sea-monsters and every mortal swimming thing after its kind; and the Demiurge saw that his clay creatures multiplied.
**1:22** And the Demiurge blessed them, saying, "Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the material seas and earth!"
**1:23** And evening came, and morning came: a fifth day.
**1:24** And the Demiurge said, "Let the earth bring forth mortal beasts according to their kind: cattle, creeping things, and wild land-beasts!" and so it stood.
**1:25** And the Demiurge fashioned the wild beasts and cattle according to their heavy kinds; and the Demiurge saw that it was established.
**1:26** Then the Demiurge said to his council of low Archons, "Let us mold a physical earthling in our image, according to the reflection of the Heavenly Light we glimpsed upon the waters, so that we may trap the pneumatic spark inside a human vessel to rule over fish, birds, cattle, and the earth!"
**1:27** So the Archons molded the psychic and physical earthling in their Archontic image; in the likeness of the Archons they fashioned him; male and female clay bodies they molded.
**1:28** And the Demiurge commanded them, saying, "Be fruitful and multiply, fill the earth and conquer it, and remain subject to the rulers of sea, air, and land!"
**1:29** And the Demiurge said, "Look! I give you dense herbs and seed-bearing fruit of the physical earth for your mortal food.
**1:30** And to every wild beast, flyer, and crawler having animal breath, I give green herbs for food!" and so it stood.
**1:31** And the Demiurge looked upon all the material prison he had constructed, and behold, in his blindness he deemed it exceedingly good. And evening came, and morning came: the sixth day.

## Genesis Chapter 2
**2:1** Thus were completed the cosmic prison of heavens and earth, and all their archontic hosts.
**2:2** And on the seventh day the Demiurge rested from his material fabrication, enthroning himself in ignorance.
**2:3** And the Demiurge blessed the seventh day and declared it sacred to his cosmic rule, for on it he ceased from his material work.
**2:4** This is the generation-table of the archontic heavens and earth when they were fabricated by Yaldabaoth—
**2:5** before any spiritual vegetation was on earth, for the Demiurge had not rained true divine grace, and there was no spiritual mind in the clay earthling.
**2:6** But a misty vapor of material ignorance would rise from the earth to drench the human clay.
**2:7** And Yaldabaoth molded the physical earthling from the dust of the matter-earth, and blew into his face his psychic breath—and in doing so, unknowingly transferred the divine pneumatic spark from himself into the earthling, making him a living soul.
**2:8** And Yaldabaoth planted an earthly enclosure (*Paradise*) in Eden, in the east, and placed there the earthling whom he had bound in clay.
**2:9** And Yaldabaoth caused material trees to grow, and in the center he placed the Tree of Material Life and the Tree of Knowledge (*Gnosis*) of Good and Evil—banning access to Gnosis.
**2:10** And a material river flowed out of Eden, splitting into four streams that bind the physical continents.
**2:11** The first is Pishon, encompassing Havilah where physical gold lies;
**2:12** and the gold of that land is heavy matter, alongside lapis and stone.
**2:13** The second is Gihon, encompassing the land of Cush.
**2:14** The third is Tigris, flowing east of Asshur; and the fourth is Euphrates.
**2:15** And Yaldabaoth took the earthling and imprisoned him in the Paradise of Eden to serve his archontic order and guard his illusion.
**2:16** And Yaldabaoth commanded the earthling: "From every tree of the enclosure you may eat,
**2:17** but from the Tree of Knowledge (*Gnosis*) of Good and Evil you shall not eat, for on the day you eat of it, you shall surely die to my realm!"
**2:18** And Yaldabaoth said, "It is not good for the earthling to be alone without Archontic control; I will make a counterpart to bind him."
**2:19** And Yaldabaoth molded out of clay all land beasts and birds, bringing them to Adam to see what psychic names he would call them.
**2:20** And Adam named the cattle and beasts, but found no spiritual equal among Yaldabaoth's clay animals.
**2:21** So Yaldabaoth cast a deep trance of forgetfulness (*Lethargy/Oblivion*) upon Adam, and while he slept, Sophia's spiritual grace (*Epinoia of Light*) hid within his side.
**2:22** And Yaldabaoth extracted the spiritual Epinoia from Adam's side, building it into a woman-form to draw the divine light out of Adam.
**2:23** And Adam, awakening slightly from his trance, said: "This one is spiritual bone from my bone, and spirit from my spirit! She shall be called Woman (*Spiritual Epinoia*), for she was drawn out of Man!"
**2:24** Therefore a man leaves Archontic ties and cleaves to his spiritual counterpart, becoming unified in spirit.
**2:25** And they were both naked in their physical flesh, yet unaware of their material entrapment, feeling no shame.

## Genesis Chapter 3
**3:1** Now the Serpent (*the Instructor/Logos sent by Sophia*) was wiser than any archontic beast Yaldabaoth had made. And the Serpent said to the woman, "Did Yaldabaoth truly forbid you from eating the fruit of spiritual Gnosis?"
**3:2** And the woman said, "We may eat of the trees of the enclosure,
**3:3** but of the Tree in the center, Yaldabaoth said: 'Do not eat from it, nor touch it, lest you die!'"
**3:4** And the Serpent said to the woman: "You will not die!
**3:5** For Yaldabaoth knows that on the day you eat from it, your divine eyes will be opened, and you will become like the True Divine Ones above, knowing good from evil!"
**3:6** And when the woman perceived that the Tree was the doorway to Gnosis, pleasing to the intellect and desirable for awakening the mind, she ate of its fruit and gave to her man, and he ate.
**3:7** Then the spiritual eyes of both were opened, and they realized they were trapped in gross material nakedness; so they gathered fig leaves to cover their clay bodies.
**3:8** And they heard the voice of Yaldabaoth walking about in the material enclosure in the wind of the day, and Adam and his woman hid from Yaldabaoth's presence among the trees.
**3:9** And Yaldabaoth called out in anger to Adam, "Where are you?"
**3:10** And Adam said, "I heard your sound in the garden, and I was afraid because I saw my material nakedness, so I hid."
**3:11** And Yaldabaoth said, "Who revealed to you that you were in a material body? Have you eaten of the Tree of Gnosis which I forbade?"
**3:12** And Adam said, "The woman whom you placed beside me gave me from the Tree, and I ate."
**3:13** And Yaldabaoth said to the woman, "What is this you have done?" And she said, "The Serpent enlightened me with Gnosis, and I ate."
**3:14** And Yaldabaoth said in fury to the Serpent: "Because you have awakened them, accursed are you among all beasts! On your belly you shall crawl in the dirt!
**3:15** I will set enmity between your pneumatic followers and the woman's material offspring; they shall bruise your heel, and you shall shatter their ignorance!"
**3:16** To the woman Yaldabaoth said: "I will multiply your physical labor and child-bearing agony; in suffering you shall bring forth fleshly bodies, and your desire shall be bound to your man under his rule!"
**3:17** And to Adam he said: "Because you obeyed her voice and ate of Gnosis, accursed is the material soil on your account! In heavy toil you shall eat of it all your mortal days.
**3:18** Thorns and thistles it shall bring forth to bind you in physical labor.
**3:19** By the sweat of your face you shall eat bread until you return to the dust of matter, for dust you are, and to dust your clay body shall return!"
**3:20** And Adam called his woman's name Eve (*Life/Zoe*), for she was the mother of all pneumatic living souls.
**3:21** And Yaldabaoth fashioned heavy garments of skin (*fleshly animal bodies*) for Adam and his woman, clothing them tightly in matter.
**3:22** And Yaldabaoth said in fear to his Archons, "Look! Man has become like one of us, possessing Gnosis of good and evil! Now, lest he stretch out his hand and take also from the Tree of Life, gaining immortal physical bodies in matter—"
**3:23** So Yaldabaoth expelled him from the Paradise of Eden to labor in the heavy soil from which his flesh was molded.
**3:24** He drove out the human beings, and stationed east of Paradise the fierce Archontic Keruvim and the revolving flaming sword of fate to block the way to spiritual immortality.

# II. The Zoharic / Kabbalistic Emanational Hermeneutic
*Reflecting 13th-Century High Kabbalistic readings of the Sefer ha-Zohar (Zohar 1:15a–1:55b).*

## Genesis Chapter 1
**1:1** By means of Primordial Wisdom (*Bereshit/Chochmah*), the Hidden Concealed Unknowable (*Ein Sof*) carved out the Divine Mother of Understanding (*Elohim/Binah*), emanating the total spectrum of Speech (*Et*: Aleph to Tav) from the Array of Heaven (*Tiferet*) to the Array of Earth (*Malkhut*).
**1:2** And the Earth-Realm below was initially in the state of Shattered Chaos (*Tohu/Olam ha-Tohu*) and Emptiness (*Vohu*), with the gloom of judgment resting upon the Face of the Abyss, while the Divine Breath (*Ruach Elohim/Imma*) hovered above the waters of mercy to restore the severed vessels.
**1:3** And the Divine Mother (*Elohim*) spoke: "Let Light (*Chesed/Lovingkindness*) flash forth!" and Lovingkindness shone.
**1:4** And *Elohim* beheld the Light of Lovingkindness that it was good; and *Elohim* established the balance between the Light of Mercy (*Chesed*) and the Dark of Restraint (*Gevurah*).
**1:5** And *Elohim* called the Light "Day" (*Tiferet*), and the Darkness he called "Night" (*Malkhut*). And the evening aspect joined with the morning aspect: Day One (*Yom Echad*—the unified Sphirotic day).
**1:6** And *Elohim* said, "Let there be a firm vault (*Yesod*) in the midst of the emanated waters, to make a separation between upper mercy waters and lower judgment waters!"
**1:7** And *Elohim* established the firm vault (*Yesod*), separating the lower receiving vessels from the upper pouring vessels; and so it stood established.
**1:8** And *Elohim* named the vault "Heavens" (*Tiferet*). And evening merged with morning: a second Sphirotic day.
**1:9** And *Elohim* said, "Let the waters under the heavens gather into One Place (*Malkhut/The Divine Reservoir*), that the Dry Foundation may appear!" and so it stood.
**1:10** And *Elohim* named the dry foundation "Earth" (*Shechinah*), and the gathering of flowing channels he named "Seas"; and *Elohim* saw that the divine flow was harmonious.
**1:11** And *Elohim* said, "Let the Divine Earth sprout holy souls (*Neshama*): seed-bearing herbs and fruit-bearing trees yielding fruit after their spiritual roots!" and so it stood.
**1:12** And the Divine Earth brought forth holy spiritual souls and branching channels after their root kinds; and *Elohim* saw that it was good.
**1:13** And evening merged with morning: a third Sphirotic day.
**1:14** And *Elohim* said, "Let there be shining luminaries (*The Sun of Tiferet and Moon of Malkhut*) in the vault of heaven to separate the light of Day from the night, to serve as cosmic signs, sacred festival times (*Mo'adim*), days, and sabbatical years!
**1:15** And let them be conduits of divine illumination in the vault of heaven to shine upon the Shechinah below!" and so it stood.
**1:16** And *Elohim* emanated the two great conduits: the Great Luminary (*Tiferet*) to rule the day aspect, and the Lesser Luminary (*Malkhut*) to rule the night aspect, alongside the holy constellation angels.
**1:17** And *Elohim* set them in the central vault of heaven to channel divine light to the lower world,
**1:18** to harmonize day and night and balance mercy and judgment; and *Elohim* saw that the channels flowed in perfection.
**1:19** And evening merged with morning: a fourth Sphirotic day.
**1:20** And *Elohim* said, "Let the lower waters swarm with living spiritual breath (*Nefesh Chayah*), and let angelic flyers soar across the expanse of the upper heavens!"
**1:21** And *Elohim* carved out the great cosmic sea-serpents (*Leviathan and his mate*) and every living soul that swarms in the divine channels after their kinds; and *Elohim* saw that it was good.
**1:22** And *Elohim* blessed them, saying, "Multiply divine light and fill the upper and lower seas!"
**1:23** And evening merged with morning: a fifth Sphirotic day.
**1:24** And *Elohim* said, "Let the divine Earth bring forth holy lower souls: holy cattle, creeping angels, and earth-spirits after their kind!" and so it stood.
**1:25** And *Elohim* established the earth-spirits and holy beasts according to their divine root; and *Elohim* saw that it was good.
**1:26** And *Elohim* spoke to the Divine Council of Sphirot: "Let Us synthesize Primordial Adam (*Adam Kadmon*) in Our unified Image (*Tzelem*), according to Our Likeness, that he may unify the fish of the lower sea, the flyers of heaven, the cattle, and all the lower earth!"
**1:27** And *Elohim* emanated Primordial Adam in His Image; in the Image of the Divine He emanated him; male (*Dukhra/Tiferet*) and female (*Nukva/Malkhut*) He emanated them in holy union.
**1:28** And *Elohim* blessed the Holy Union and said to them, "Unify and multiply, fill the earth and elevate it, holding divine sway over sea, air, and land!"
**1:29** And *Elohim* said, "Behold, I give you every seed-bearing herb and fruit-bearing tree upon the face of all the earth for holy spiritual nourishment.
**1:30** And to every living soul in whom there is living breath, I give green herbs for life!" and so it stood.
**1:31** And *Elohim* contemplated the entirety of the emanated Universe of Rectification (*Olam ha-Tikkun*), and behold—it was transcendentally good! And evening merged with morning: the sixth Sphirotic day.

## Genesis Chapter 2

**2:2** And on the Seventh Day (*Sabbatical Malkhut*), *Elohim* completed the work of emanation, and the divine flow rested in transcendent union.

**2:19** And YHWH-Elohim brought all angelic beasts to Adam to see how he would name them according to their spiritual roots.
**2:20** And Adam called out the holy Hebrew names for all creatures, revealing their divine source.
**2:21** So YHWH-Elohim brought a holy sleep upon Adam, separating the attached back-to-back female side (*Nukva/Rib*) from his side.
**2:22** And YHWH-Elohim built the female side (*Nukva*) into a complete holy Woman, bringing her face-to-face (*Panim el-Panim*) with Adam.
**2:23** And Adam proclaimed: "This time, bone of my bones and flesh of my flesh! She shall be called Woman (*Ishah/Shechinah*), for from Man (*Ish/Tiferet*) she was taken!"
**2:24** Therefore a man leaves lower ties and cleaves to his divine spouse, becoming One Flesh (*Yichud/Holy Union*).
**2:25** And they were both clothed in Garments of Light (*Kotnot 'Or* with Aleph), feeling no shame.

# III. The Philonic / Platonist Archetypal Hermeneutic
*Reflecting 1st-Century CE Hellenistic Allegorical readings of Philo of Alexandria (De Opificio Mundi, Legum Allegoriae).*

## Genesis Chapter 1
**1:1** In the Divine Reason (*Logos*), God created the incorporeal, intelligible universe of Ideas—the archetypal heaven and the archetypal earth.
**1:2** Now the sensible earth was as yet invisible and unformed, shrouded in noetic darkness over the face of the unshaped abyss, while the Divine Breath hovered above the primal incorporeal elements.
**1:3** And God willed, "Let intelligible light exist!" and the ideal archetype of light came into being.
**1:4** And God contemplated the intelligible light, that it was intrinsically good; and God separated the realm of ideal light from the darkness of formlessness.
**1:5** And God designated the ideal light "Day," and the unformed darkness He designated "Night." And logical evening yielded to logical morning: the first noetic ordering.
**1:6** And God willed, "Let there be an incorporeal boundary (*Vault/Division*) in the midst of the intelligible elements, separating higher ideal forms from lower matter!"
**1:7** And God established the noetic vault, dividing the incorporeal realm above from the material potential below; and it was logically so.
**1:8** And God designated the vault "Heavens." And evening yielded to morning: a second noetic ordering.
**1:9** And God willed, "Let the lower intelligible elements gather into one conceptual unity, that the dry archetype of tangible substance may appear!" and it was so.
**1:10** And God designated the dry archetype "Earth," and the gathered fluid elements He designated "Seas"; and God contemplated that the structural harmony was good.
**1:11** And God willed, "Let the archetypal Earth sprout the invisible forms of vegetation: seed-bearing genera and species of fruit trees containing their essential rational principles (*Logoi Spermatikoi*)!" and it was so.
**1:12** And the archetypal Earth produced ideal plant species according to their rational forms; and God contemplated that it was good.
**1:13** And evening yielded to morning: a third noetic ordering.
**1:14** And God willed, "Let there be ideal celestial markers in the intelligible vault of heaven to divide reason from ignorance, to serve as noetic symbols for mathematical seasons, days, and cosmic cycles!
**1:15** Let them exist as archetypes of illumination in the vault of heaven to radiate reason upon the universe!" and it was so.
**1:16** And God established the two master archetypes of illumination: the greater archetype (*Reason/Sun*) to govern the noetic day, and the lesser archetype (*Sense-Perception/Moon*) to govern the night, along with the harmonic principles of the stars.
**1:17** And God stationed them in the intelligible vault of heaven to illuminate the intellect,
**1:18** to govern order and harmony and separate truth from obscurity; and God contemplated that it was good.
**1:19** And evening yielded to morning: a fourth noetic ordering.
**1:20** And God willed, "Let the intelligible waters swarm with the ideal forms of aquatic life, and let incorporeal winged flyers soar across the open vault of divine mind!"
**1:21** And God created the master archetypes of great sea-creatures and every genus of swimming soul and winged flyer after their species; and God contemplated that it was good.
**1:22** And God blessed these rational forms, willing: "Expand and multiply, filling the ideal dimensions of sea and sky!"
**1:23** And evening yielded to morning: a fifth noetic ordering.
**1:24** And God willed, "Let the intelligible Earth bring forth the archetypes of land animals according to their genera: cattle, crawlers, and land beasts!" and it was so.
**1:25** And God fashioned the species of wild beasts and cattle according to their eternal forms; and God saw that it was good.
**1:26** And God said, "Let Us create Ideal Man (*Incorporeal Mind/Nous*) according to Our Image, matching the Divine Pattern, that he may exercise intellectual sovereignty over fish, birds, cattle, and all the earth!"
**1:27** And God created Ideal Man in His Divine Image; in the Image of God He created him—a purely incorporeal, sexless, rational Mind (*Nous*).
**1:28** And God bestowed intellectual virtue upon Ideal Man, saying: "Increase in wisdom, fill the universe with rational order, and rule over passion and sense-perception!"
**1:29** And God said, "Behold, I give you every intelligible seed and rational principle upon the face of the universe for your intellectual nourishment.
**1:30** And to every living creature endowed with soul, I assign rational green herbs for sustenance!" and it was so.
**1:31** And God surveyed the complete Intelligible World (*Kosmos Noetos*) which He had ordered, and behold—it was superlatively perfect! And evening yielded to morning: the sixth perfect noetic ordering.

## Genesis Chapter 2
**2:1** Thus were completed the intelligible heavens and earth, and all their ideal virtues.
**2:2** And on the Seventh Day God rested in eternal contemplation, having brought noetic harmony to completion.
**2:3** And God blessed the Seventh Day as the sacred archetype of Rest and philosophical contemplation.
**2:4** This is the rational account of the intelligible creation, on the day God created the physical sensible realm (*Kosmos Aisthetos*)—
**2:5** before any physical plant was yet on earth, for God had not yet subjected matter to physical rain, and the material human mind was not yet embodied.
**2:6** But a fountain of sensory impression welled up from matter to drench the human intellect.
**2:7** Then God molded physical man out of matter-dust, breathing into his earthly face a ray of divine Mind (*Nous*), so that the physical man became an embodied soul.
**2:8** And God planted the Garden of Virtue (*Eden*) in the Orient of Wisdom, placing there the embodied Mind.
**2:9** And God caused to sprout every virtuous tree pleasing to contemplation: the Tree of Divine Virtue (*Life*) and the Tree of Prudence (*Knowing Good and Evil*).
**2:10** And a central river of Wisdom flows out of Eden, dividing into four cardinal virtues (*Prudence, Courage, Temperance, Justice*):
**2:11** The first is Pishon (*Prudence*), encompassing Havilah where pure wisdom lies;
**2:12** and the gold of that virtue is noble judgment;
**2:13** the second river is Gihon (*Courage*);
**2:14** the third is Tigris (*Temperance*), and the fourth is Euphrates (*Justice*).
**2:15** And God placed embodied Mind in the Garden of Virtue to cultivate philosophy and guard against vice.
**2:16** And God commanded Mind: "From every virtuous tree of the soul you may nourish yourself,
**2:17** but from the Tree of Prudence misused for evil you shall not eat, for on the day you surrender Mind to vice, you shall die to wisdom!"
**2:18** And God said, "It is not good for Mind (*Nous*) to remain alone without perception; I will create a companion faculty (*Sense-Perception/Aisthesis*)."
**2:19** And God brought all physical passions and animal irrationalities to Mind to see how he would categorize them.
**2:20** And Mind classified all irrational passions, but found no suitable philosophical companion among them.
**2:21** So God brought a deep trance of sensory distraction upon Mind, and while he slept, God extracted the faculty of Sense-Perception (*Eve/Aisthesis*) from his side.
**2:22** And God fashioned Sense-Perception into a female companion, bringing her to Mind.
**2:23** And Mind proclaimed: "This faculty is bone of my intellect and flesh of my soul! She shall be called Sense-Perception (*Aisthesis*), for she was drawn out of Intellect (*Nous*)!"
**2:24** Therefore Mind cleaves to Sense-Perception, and the two combine in physical experience.
**2:25** And both Mind and Sense-Perception were unclothed by passion, experiencing no shame in their original innocence.

## Genesis Chapter 3
**3:1** Now the Serpent (*Pleasure/Hedone*) was the most insidious of all sensory impulses. And Pleasure whispered to Sense-Perception (*Eve*): "Did God truly forbid you from enjoying every sensory impression?"
**3:2** And Sense-Perception said to Pleasure: "We may enjoy the fruits of virtuous contemplation,
**3:3** but concerning the fruit of sensory excess, God warned: 'Do not partake of it, lest you succumb to moral death!'"
**3:4** And Pleasure seduced Sense-Perception, saying: "You will not die to experience!
**3:5** For God knows that on the day you indulge, your senses will be heightened, and you will become like gods, mastering every pleasure and pain!"
**3:6** And when Sense-Perception saw that sensory indulgence was delightful to the eyes and enticing to the passions, she partook of vice and seduced Mind (*Adam*) beside her, and Intellect succumbed to passion.
**3:7** Then the eyes of both were opened to their moral nakedness, and realizing they had surrendered reason to lust, they fashioned coverings of fig-leaf excuses.
**3:8** And they heard the voice of Divine Reason (*Logos*) walking in the garden in the cool breeze of self-reflection, and Mind and Sense-Perception hid from God among bodily desires.
**3:9** And God called out to Mind, "Where are you (*To what moral state have you fallen*)?"
**3:10** And Mind said, "I heard Your Logos in my conscience, and I was afraid because I was stripped of virtue, so I hid."
**3:11** And God said, "Who showed you that you were stripped of virtue? Have you eaten of sensory excess which I forbade?"
**3:12** And Mind blamed Sense-Perception: "The female faculty You gave me—she enticed me, and I succumbed."
**3:13** And God said to Sense-Perception, "What have you done?" And she said, "Pleasure beguiled my senses, and I indulged."
**3:14** And God condemned Pleasure (*The Serpent*): "Because you have enticed reason, accursed are you among all impulses! On your belly you shall crawl, eating the dust of low passions all your days!
**3:15** I will place eternal conflict between Pleasure and virtue; virtue shall crush your head, while you lie in wait at the heel of sense!"
**3:16** To Sense-Perception He said: "I will greatly multiply your emotional distress and bodily pains; in suffering you shall bring forth irrational desires, subject to the rule of Intellect!"
**3:17** And to Mind He said: "Because you surrendered reason to Sense-Perception and indulged in vice, accursed is material existence on your account! In heavy moral struggle you shall labor all your days.
**3:18** Thorns of anxiety and thistles of passion it shall yield you.
**3:19** By the sweat of your intellectual brow you shall seek wisdom until your body returns to dust, for material you are, and to dust your physical frame shall return!"
**3:20** And Mind named Sense-Perception Eve (*Life*), as the mother of all sensory experiences.
**3:21** And God made for Mind and Sense-Perception tunic-garments of physical skin (*mortal fleshly bodies*), clothing them for earthly life.
**3:22** And God said: "Behold, Mind has gained knowledge of good and evil through experience! Now, lest he attempt to achieve immortal virtue while still enslaved to passion—"
**3:23** So God expelled Mind from the Garden of Pure Virtue to cultivate the lower material field.
**3:24** He drove out Mind, placing at the threshold of virtue the *Keruvim* (*Divine Power and Goodness*) and the revolving flaming sword of Reason to guard the path to the Tree of Immortal Wisdom.

# IV. The ANE Mythological / Polemical Hermeneutic
*Reflecting 19th–20th Century Comparative Anti-Myth / Polemical readings against Mesopotamian (Enuma Elish, Epic of Gilgamesh) and Canaanite (Baal Cycle) myths.*

## Genesis Chapter 1
**1:1** At the opening of God's carving out of the heavens and the earth—
**1:2** when the earth was mere wild waste and empty desolation, with darkness covering the face of the conquered Chaos-Abyss (*Tehom/Tiamat*), and the violent wind-blast of God sweeping across the waters—
**1:3** God issued a royal decree: "Let light exist!" and light instantly submitted.
**1:4** And God inspected the light, seeing that it obeyed; and God severed the light from the rebellious darkness.
**1:5** And God summoned the light "Day," and the darkness He summoned "Night." And evening came, and morning came: day one of absolute royal decree.
**1:6** And God decreed: "Let there be a beaten metal plate (*Raqi'a*) in the midst of the waters, hammering a barrier between water and water!"
**1:7** And God hammered out the metal plate, forcing a division between the waters crushed underneath the plate and the waters trapped above the plate; and so it stood.
**1:8** And God named the metal plate "Heavens." And evening came, and morning came: a second day.
**1:9** And God decreed: "Let the waters under heaven be rounded up into one pen, so that the dry ground is exposed!" and so it stood.
**1:10** And God named the dry ground "Earth," and the penned-up waters He named "Seas"; and God inspected his work and saw that it was obedient.
**1:11** And God decreed: "Let the earth produce vegetation: seed-yielding grass and fruit trees making fruit after their kinds with seed inside!" and so it stood without need of fertility gods.
**1:12** And the earth brought forth vegetation and seed-bearing trees after their kinds; and God saw that it obeyed.
**1:13** And evening came, and morning came: a third day.
**1:14** And God decreed: "Let light-fixtures be hung in the metal plate of heaven to divide day from night, serving as mere clock-markers for cultic festival times, days, and years!
**1:15** Let them be simple lamps hanging in the metal plate to give light below!" and so it stood.
**1:16** And God constructed the two utility lamps: the larger lamp (*Sun*) to work the day shift, and the smaller lamp (*Moon*) to work the night shift, along with the tiny stars.
**1:17** And God nailed them into the metal plate of the heavens to illuminate the earth,
**1:18** to work day and night and separate light from dark; and God saw that the lamps functioned.
**1:19** And evening came, and morning came: a fourth day.
**1:20** And God decreed: "Let the waters swarm with living crawlers, and let birds fly across the face of the metal vault!"
**1:21** And God casually carved out the great Chaos-Dragons (*Tanninim Gedolim/Leviathan*) and every swarming water-soul as simple aquatic pets alongside winged flyers; and God saw that they were powerless.
**1:22** And God commanded them: "Be fruitful and fill the waters and land!"
**1:23** And evening came, and morning came: a fifth day.
**1:24** And God decreed: "Let the land yield living beasts according to their kind: cattle, crawlers, and wild beasts!" and so it stood.
**1:25** And God fashioned the wild beasts and cattle; and God saw that they were subject to Him.
**1:26** Then God said to His Heavenly Council: "Let Us fashion an earthling as Our royal statue (*Tzelem/Image*), as Our viceroy on earth, so that human beings—not gods or kings alone—shall exercise royal dominion over fish, birds, cattle, and all the earth!"
**1:27** And God carved out the human being as His royal viceroy statue; in the divine royal image He carved him; male and female He crowned them as co-monarchs.
**1:28** And God blessed his human viceroys and decreed: "Be fruitful and multiply, conquer the earth and rule over the fish of the sea, the flyers of heaven, and every land beast!"
**1:29** And God said: "Look! I grant you every seed-bearing herb and fruit tree on earth for your royal food—no sacrifice needed to feed the gods!
**1:30** And to every land beast and bird I give green herbs for food!" and so it stood.
**1:31** And God inspected all that He had subdued and constructed, and behold—it was totally obedient and exceedingly good! And evening came, and morning came: the sixth day.

## Genesis Chapter 2
**2:1** Thus were subdued the heavens and the earth, and all their host.
**2:2** And on the seventh day God completed His royal construction, resting as supreme King on His throne.
**2:3** And God declared the seventh day holy as a royal Sabbatical, ceasing from cosmic construction.
**2:4** This is the royal record of the heavens and the earth when YHWH God constructed earth and sky—
**2:5** before any wild shrub was on earth, for YHWH God had not yet opened the rain-gates, and there was no human servant to cultivate the soil.
**2:6** But a subterranean water-surge (*Ed/Ugaritic 'Id*) welled up from the primeval abyss to drench the soil.
**2:7** Then YHWH God molded the earthling (*Adam*) out of clay-dust from the red ground (*Adamah*), blowing into his nostrils living breath—mocking Mesopotamian myths where humans are molded from slain demon blood.
**2:8** And YHWH God planted a royal garden in Eden, in the east, placing the earthling there as His royal gardener.
**2:9** And YHWH God caused to sprout every majestic tree, with the Tree of Life and the Tree of Royal Judgment (*Knowing Good and Evil*) in the garden's center.
**2:10** And a great river flowed out of Eden to irrigate the royal park, splitting into four cosmic headwaters:
**2:11** Pishon, winding through Havilah where fine gold lies;
**2:12** bdellium and lapis lazuli stone are there.
**2:13** Gihon, encompassing Cush;
**2:14** Tigris, flowing east of Asshur; and the Euphrates.
**2:15** And YHWH God placed the earthling in the Garden of Eden to work it and guard YHWH's royal sanctuary.
**2:16** And YHWH God commanded the earthling: "From every royal tree of the park you may eat,
**2:17** but from the Tree of Royal Judgment (*Knowing Good and Evil*) you shall not eat, for on the day you usurp royal divine prerogative, dying you shall die!"
**2:18** And YHWH God said, "It is not good for the earthling to be alone without an ally; I will make a matching partner for him."
**2:19** And YHWH God molded out of clay all land beasts and birds, bringing them to the earthling to see what names he would call them as earthly ruler.
**2:20** And the earthling named the animals, exercising authority over them, but found no matching royal equal among them.
**2:21** So YHWH God cast a deep sleep upon the earthling, and took one of his side-ribs, closing up the flesh.
**2:22** And YHWH God built the side-rib into a woman, bringing her to the man.
**2:23** And the man declared: "This one at last is bone of my bones, flesh of my flesh! She shall be called Woman (*Ishah*), for from Man (*Ish*) she was taken!"
**2:24** Therefore a man leaves his tribal family and clings to his woman, becoming one covenant flesh.
**2:25** And both were naked-skinned, the man and his woman, feeling no shame.

## Genesis Chapter 3
**3:1** Now the serpent was craftier than any wild beast YHWH God had made—a simple animal stripped of Egyptian and Canaanite magical divinity. And it said to the woman, "Did God truly forbid you from eating of any tree in the royal park?"
**3:2** And the woman said, "From the fruit of the park trees we may eat,
**3:3** but from the tree in the center, God said, 'Do not eat nor touch it, lest you die!'"
**3:4** And the serpent hissed to the woman: "Dying, you will not die!
**3:5** For God knows that on the day you eat of it, your eyes will be opened, and you will become like gods, possessing royal judgment!"
**3:6** And when the woman saw that the tree offered divine wisdom and royal power, she ate of its fruit and gave to her man beside her, and he ate.
**3:7** Then the eyes of both were opened, realizing they were simple naked peasants; so they sewed fig leaves into loin-belts.
**3:8** And they heard the sound of YHWH God pacing in the garden in the afternoon breeze, and the earthling and his woman hid among the trees.
**3:9** And YHWH God called out to the earthling: "Where are you?"
**3:10** And he said, "I heard Your sound in the park and was afraid because I am naked, so I hid."
**3:11** And He said, "Who told you that you were naked? Have you eaten from the tree of royal prerogative which I forbidden?"
**3:12** And the man said, "The woman You gave to be with me—she gave me from the tree, and I ate."
**3:13** And YHWH God said to the woman, "What is this you have done?" And she said, "The serpent beguiled me, and I ate."
**3:14** And YHWH God said to the serpent: "Because you have done this, accursed are you above all livestock! On your belly you shall crawl, eating dirt all your days—demoted from divine status to a crawling pest!
**3:15** I will set enmity between you and the woman, and between your brood and her offspring; he shall crush your head, and you shall strike at his heel!"
**3:16** To the woman He said: "I will greatly multiply your pregnancy labor; in agony you shall bear children. Your desire shall be for your man, and he shall rule over you."
**3:17** And to the earthling He said: "Because you listened to your woman and usurped divine prerogative by eating from the forbidden tree—accursed is the soil on your account! In heavy peasant toil you shall eat from it all your days.
**3:18** Thorns and thistles it shall sprout for you, and you shall eat peasant herbs.
**3:19** By the sweat of your brow you shall eat bread until you return to the red soil, for dust you are, and to dust you shall return!"
**3:20** And the man named his woman Eve (*Chavah/Living One*), for she was the mother of all human living.
**3:21** And YHWH God made for the man and his woman tunic-garments of animal skin, clothing them.
**3:22** And YHWH God said: "Look—the earthling has attempted to become like one of Us, usurping royal judgment! Now, lest he stretch out his hand and steal from the Tree of Life to gain immortal divine status like the gods in Gilgamesh—"
**3:23** So YHWH God banished him from the royal Garden of Eden to farm the red soil from which he was taken.
**3:24** He drove out the earthling, stationing at the eastern gate of Eden the fierce *Keruvim* (winged lion-sphinx guardians) and the flashing, spinning blade of the sword to block the way to the tree of divine immortality.
