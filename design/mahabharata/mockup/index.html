IV

The Hall

Sabhā Parva 2.59–63

From Duryodhana’s order through Draupadī’s question to the granting of the boons.

A shout goes up praising Vikarṇa and reviling Śakuni. When it dies down, Karṇa answers, and his answer is the true voice of the hall.

35
एको भर्ता स्त्रिया देवैर्विहितः कुरुनन्दन ।eko bhartā striyā devair vihitaḥ kurunandana इयं त्वनेकवशगा बन्धकीति विनिश्चिता ॥ ३५ ॥iyaṃ tv anekavaśagā bandhakīti viniścitā

One husband is ordained for a woman by the gods, son of Kuru; but this one is in the power of many, and is therefore determined to be a whore.

Apparatus 1
  1. 35dबन्धकीbandhakī]Notea woman “tied to” many men (a guess from the root; the attested sense is unchaste woman, harlot). Bhīma uses the plural bandhakyaḥ earlier (2.61.1). Karṇa’s argument runs: the gods ordain one husband; she is subject to many; so she is a bandhakī. How coarse the word was is uncertain. The blunt English is kept because the speech is meant to degrade.

ततो दुःशासनो राजन्द्रौपद्या वसनं बलात् ।tato duḥśāsano rājan draupadyā vasanaṃ balāt सभामध्ये समाक्षिप्य व्यपक्रष्टुं प्रचक्रमे ॥ ४० ॥sabhāmadhye samākṣipya vyapakraṣṭuṃ pracakrame

“Then Duḥśāsana, king, seizing Draupadī’s garment by force in the middle of the hall, began to pull it off.

41
आकृष्यमाणे वसने द्रौपद्यास्तु विशां पते ।ākṛṣyamāṇe vasane draupadyās tu viśāṃ pate तद्रूपमपरं वस्त्रं प्रादुरासीदनेकशः ॥ ४१ ॥tadrūpam aparaṃ vastraṃ prādurāsīd anekaśaḥ

And as Draupadī’s garment was being pulled, lord of peoples, another garment of the same kind appeared — again and again, many times over.

Apparatus 1
  1. 41dप्रादुरासीद्prādurāsīd]Note“appeared.” In the constituted text the miracle has no stated cause: Draupadī does not pray, and no god is named when the garments appear. In the hall scene, the only mention of Kṛṣṇa before it is Duḥśāsana’s taunt at 2.60.26. The starred insertions add prayers and credit Dharma; how they spread across the recensions is not settled here.
