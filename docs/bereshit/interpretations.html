The text of Genesis 1–4 has never existed as a single static monument. Across millennia, radically opposing hermeneutical traditions have read its syntax, roots, and narrative arc through entirely different cosmological and metaphysical lenses. This collection presents five complete translation rewrites, each accompanied by heavy historical, philological, and theological commentary explaining its underlying mechanics.

1. The Davidic-Era Oral Performance (c. 10th Century BCE — Genesis 1:1–4:18 Complete)

Context: Early Monarchic Judah · Northwest Semitic Oral Bards · ANE Cultic Resonance · All 98 Verses

Linguistic and Cultural Mechanics of 10th-Century BCE Israel

To hear Genesis 1:1–4:18 as it sounded in the court of David haMelech in Hebron or Jerusalem is to enter a world structured by clan blood-feud (go'el ha-dam), sacred sanctuary boundary stones, royal-sacred gardens, and animal tribute offerings (minchah). In 10th-century BCE Hebrew, abstract Latinate theology did not exist; vocabulary was physical, concrete, and kinetic.

Parataxis and Rhythmic Cadence: The narrative is driven by the vav-consecutive (vayyomer, vayya'as, vayyera), creating an incessant oral driving beat ("And... and... and...") suited for a moshid (bard) singing accompanied by lyre (kinnor) or harp (nevel).

Physical Root Meaning: Bara is not creatio ex nihilo, but cutting, carving, and clearing out space (cognate to beri'ah in Josh 17:15). Raqi'a is a physical metal vault hammered out (raqa) by a smith to keep back upper cosmic waters. Tehom is the roaring subterranean abyss (cognate to Akkadian Tiamat). Nefesh chayah is a living breathing throat. Adam / Adamah is the "earthling" molded from "red soil". Neshamah is a blast of living breath breathed directly into the nostrils ('appayim). 'Atzem me'atzamay ("Bone of my bones, flesh of my flesh") is a sacred tribal/clan covenant oath formula (cf. 2 Sam 5:1). Chatat in 4:7 is a rovetz—a crouching threshold demon (Akkadian rabisu).

Anthropological & Ritual Reflection: In 10th-century Judah, murder (damim, plural "bloods") was a physical pollution of the land. The red soil (adamah) physically swallows the spilled blood, rendering the earth barren to the perpetrator and forcing him into outlawry (Nod/Roaming). The mark ('ot) functions as an ancient clan sign of asylum, enforcing blood-vengeance restrictions.

The Cosmic Inverse Reading

In Sethian and Valentinian Gnostic hermeneutics, Genesis 1–3 is read as an inverse allegory detailing the creation of a material prison by an ignorant, arrogant Demiurge named Yaldabaoth (Saklas or Samael), who was born from a flaw in the divine Pleroma (Sophia). Rather than praising the creator, this tradition demotes Elohim to a blind, tyrant-demon who steals divine light particles and traps them inside physical clay bodies (adam).

Reversal of Roles: The creator's prohibition against eating from the Tree of Knowledge (Gnosis) is an attempt to keep humanity in a state of spiritual stupor and oblivion. The Serpent is not a devil, but the heroic instructor—a manifestation of Sophia or the Logos—sent from the higher Pleroma to give Gnosis, open human spiritual eyes, and liberate humanity from Yaldabaoth's material illusion.

3. The Zoharic / Kabbalistic Emanational Hermeneutic (Genesis 1–3 Complete)

Context: 13th-Century High Kabbalah · Sefer ha-Zohar · Sphirotic Unfolding

Intra-Divine Dynamics in Ein Sof

In the Zohar (Zohar 1:15a), Genesis 1–3 does not describe the external creation of physical dirt or trees. It describes the internal, intra-divine emanation of the Unknowable Infinite (Ein Sof) into the ten divine attributes (Sphirot).

Syntactic Shift in 1:1: The Zohar reads Bereshit bara Elohim with a radical grammatical shift: "By means of Bereshit [Chochmah/Wisdom], IT [Ein Sof] carved out Elohim [Binah/Understanding]!" Elohim is the direct object, not the subject! The opening verse recounts how the Unknowable Concealed Spark etched out the Divine Mother Binah.

The Fall as Uncoupling: In Genesis 2–3, Adam ha-Rishon represents the synthesis of all worlds. Eve (Nukva/Shechinah) was originally emanated attached back-to-back (Achor be-Achor) with Adam (Tiferet) before being separated for face-to-face union (Panim el-Panim). The sin of eating from the Tree of Knowledge was the uncoupling of the fruit from its root—severing the Shechinah from the upper Sphirot and plunging the world into exile.

1:1By means of Primordial Wisdom (*Bereshit/Chochmah*), the Hidden Concealed Unknowable (*Ein Sof*) carved out the Divine Mother of Understanding (*Elohim/Binah*), emanating the total spectrum of Speech (*Et*: Aleph to Tav) from the Array of Heaven (*Tiferet*) to the Array of Earth (*Malkhut*).

2:8And the Lord God planted a paradise in Eden, in the east, and placed there the man whom he had molded.
2:9And God caused to spring up out of the earth every timber beautiful to sight and fine for eating, and the tree of life in the midst of the paradise, and the tree of knowing that which is fine and that which is evil.
2:10And a river goes out of Eden to water the paradise, and from there it separates into four head-waters:
2:11The name of the first is Pishon; it encompasses the whole land of Havilah, where the gold is;
2:12and the gold of that land is fine; carbuncle and the emerald stone are there.
2:13The name of the second river is Gihon; it encompasses the whole land of Ethiopia.
2:14The name of the third river is Tigris, going opposite Assyria; and the fourth river is Euphrates.
2:15And the Lord God took the man whom he had made and set him in the paradise of delight to cultivate it and guard it.
2:16And the Lord God commanded Adam, saying: "From every timber in the paradise you may eat for food,
2:17but from the tree of knowing fine and evil you shall not eat from it; on whatever day you eat from it, dying you shall die!"
2:18And the Lord God said, "It is not fine that man should be alone; let us make for him a helper corresponding to him!"
2:19And God molded further out of the earth all the wild beasts of the field and all the flyers of the heaven, and led them to Adam to see what he would call them; and whatever Adam called every living soul, that was its name.
2:20And Adam gave names to all the cattle, to all the flyers of the heaven, and to all the wild beasts of the field; but for Adam there was not found a helper corresponding to him.
2:21And God cast an ecstasy (*Ekstasis/Deep Trance*) upon Adam, and he slept; and he took one of his ribs and filled up flesh instead of it.
2:22And the Lord God built the rib which he took from Adam into a woman, and led her to Adam.
2:23And Adam said, "This now is bone out of my bones, and flesh out of my flesh; she shall be called Woman (*Gyne*), because out of her husband (*Aner*) she was taken."
2:24On this account a man shall leave his father and his mother and be joined to his wife, and the two shall become into one flesh.
2:25And the two were naked, both Adam and his wife, and they were not ashamed.
3:1Now the serpent was the most prudent of all the beasts upon the earth which the Lord God had made. And the serpent said to the woman, "Why is it that God said, 'You shall not eat from any timber of the paradise'?"
3:2And the woman said to the serpent, "From the fruit of the timber of the paradise we may eat,
3:3but from the fruit of the tree which is in the midst of the paradise, God said: 'You shall not eat from it, nor shall you touch it, lest you die!'"
3:4And the serpent said to the woman, "By no means shall you die by death!
3:5For God knows that in whatever day you eat from it, your eyes will be opened, and you will be as gods, knowing fine and evil!"
3:6And the woman saw that the tree was fine for eating, and pleasing for the eyes to see, and beautiful to contemplate; and taking of its fruit she ate, and gave also to her husband with her, and they ate.
3:7And the eyes of the two were opened, and they knew that they were naked; and they sewed fig-leaves together and made for themselves waist-girdles.
3:8And they heard the voice of the Lord God walking in the paradise in the afternoon breeze, and Adam and his wife hid from the face of the Lord God in the midst of the timber of the paradise.
3:9And the Lord God called Adam and said to him, "Adam, where are you?"
3:10And he said, "I heard your voice as you walked in the paradise, and I was afraid because I am naked, and I hid."
3:11And he said to him, "Who announced to you that you were naked, unless you ate from the timber of which alone I commanded you not to eat?"
3:12And Adam said, "The woman whom you gave to be with me—she gave me from the timber, and I ate."
3:13And the Lord God said to the woman, "Why have you done this?" And the woman said, "The serpent deceived me, and I ate."
3:14And the Lord God said to the serpent, "Because you have done this, accursed are you above all the cattle and above all the wild beasts upon the earth; upon your chest and belly you shall go, and earth you shall eat all the days of your life!
3:15And I will put enmity between you and the woman, and between your seed and her seed; he shall watch for your head, and you shall watch for his heel!"
3:16And to the woman he said, "I will greatly multiply your pain and your groaning; in pains you shall bring forth children, and your turning shall be to your husband, and he shall rule over you!"
3:17And to Adam he said, "Because you listened to the voice of your wife and ate from the timber of which alone I commanded you not to eat from it—accursed is the earth in your labor! In pains you shall eat it all the days of your life.
3:18Thorns and caltrops it shall send up for you, and you shall eat the grass of the field.
3:19In the sweat of your face you shall eat your bread until you return into the earth out of which you were taken; for earth you are, and into earth you shall depart!"
3:20And Adam called the name of his wife "Life" (*Zoe*), because she was mother of all the living.
3:21And the Lord God made for Adam and for his wife coats of leather (*Chitonas dermatinous*), and clothed them.
3:22And God said, "Behold, Adam has become as one out of us, to know fine and evil! And now, lest at any time he stretch forth his hand and take from the tree of life, and eat, and live into the age—"
3:23And the Lord God sent him forth out of the paradise of delight to cultivate the earth out of which he was taken.
3:24And he cast out Adam and settled him opposite the paradise of delight, and stationed the Cherubim and the flaming sword that turns to guard the way of the tree of life.
